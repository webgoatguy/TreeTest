How to play
  • Move order is 1‑2‑2‑2‑2… Blue places one piece first, then each side alternates placing two pieces per turn.
  • Pick a piece with 1–5. Press the same number again to rotate it.
  • A piece must touch your own edge (top for red, bottom for blue) or one of your existing pieces, sharing a full side — not just a corner.
  • If your new piece touches an opponent piece, the larger of the two is destroyed. Equal-sized pieces simply touch — nothing happens.
  • Destroying a piece also destroys any of that player's pieces that are no longer connected back to their edge.
  • You may not place a piece that would immediately be destroyed — the game blocks that click.
  • First to 100 total area wins.
  • Enter or the Place button confirms a placement at the hovered square (useful on touch — tap to preview, then tap Place). U undoes a move.
  • Switch "Opponent" to Computer to play against a bot, pick which color it controls and a difficulty, then press New game.
  • Switch "Opponent" to Online, create a room, and send the room link to a friend. Each of you picks a color; moves sync automatically every second or two. Undo isn't available online, since it would rewrite a move your opponent already saw.
